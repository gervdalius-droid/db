One job, start to finish — nothing retyped

The five apps hand work to each other. A phone call becomes a quote, a quote becomes a project, a project becomes a cutting plan — and the finished job gets invoiced in the same workspace.

  1. CRM The enquiry lands Client, contact and what they want — on the board with a next step and a date.
  3. Offer Price it The client carries over. Cost the cabinets, set your margin, send the PDF.
  5. DB Build it An accepted quote opens a project with its steps, deadline and team already filled in.
  7. Nesting Cut it Parts go straight to the saw, offcuts back to the shared warehouse.
  9. Invoices Bill it The delivered job becomes a VAT invoice and a waybill — and then a payment you can chase.

Use one app on its own, or the whole chain. The hand-offs only appear once you have both ends.
